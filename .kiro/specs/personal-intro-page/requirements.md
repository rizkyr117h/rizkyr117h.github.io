# Requirements Document

## Introduction

A personal introduction page that showcases Rizky Ramdhan Nugraha's professional profile as a Site Reliability Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and Cloud Engineer. The page will present his technical expertise, experience, and technology stack in a professional and engaging manner.

## Glossary

- **Personal_Page**: The web-based introduction page displaying professional information
- **Profile_Section**: A dedicated area displaying personal and professional summary
- **Experience_Section**: A section showcasing work history and achievements
- **Tech_Stack_Section**: A display area for technical skills and technologies
- **Visitor**: A person viewing the personal introduction page

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Professional Profile Display

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want to see Rizky's professional identity and summary, so that I can quickly understand his expertise and background.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Personal_Page SHALL display "Rizky Ramdhan Nugraha" as the primary name
2. THE Personal_Page SHALL show his professional titles: "Site Reliability Engineer / DevOps Engineer / Cloud Engineer"
3. THE Personal_Page SHALL include a professional profile picture
4. THE Personal_Page SHALL include a professional summary section
5. THE Personal_Page SHALL present information in a clean, readable format

### Requirement 2: Experience Showcase

**User Story:** As a potential employer or collaborator, I want to learn about Rizky's professional experience, so that I can assess his qualifications and background.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Personal_Page SHALL provide a dedicated experience section
2. WHEN experience information is provided, THE Personal_Page SHALL display it in chronological or relevance order
3. THE Personal_Page SHALL accommodate future experience updates without structural changes
4. THE Personal_Page SHALL present experience information in a scannable format

### Requirement 3: Technology Stack Display

**User Story:** As a technical recruiter or peer, I want to see Rizky's technology stack and skills, so that I can understand his technical capabilities.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Personal_Page SHALL display a comprehensive technology stack section
2. THE Personal_Page SHALL organize technologies by categories (e.g., Cloud Platforms, Tools, Languages)
3. THE Personal_Page SHALL present the tech stack in a visually appealing manner
4. THE Personal_Page SHALL accommodate additions to the technology stack without layout issues

### Requirement 4: Responsive Design

**User Story:** As a visitor using various devices, I want the page to display properly on my device, so that I can access the information regardless of screen size.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Personal_Page SHALL display correctly on desktop browsers
2. THE Personal_Page SHALL display correctly on tablet devices
3. THE Personal_Page SHALL display correctly on mobile devices
4. THE Personal_Page SHALL maintain readability across all supported screen sizes

### Requirement 5: Professional Presentation

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the page to look professional and polished, so that I can trust the quality of Rizky's work based on his presentation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Personal_Page SHALL use a professional color scheme and typography
2. THE Personal_Page SHALL maintain consistent styling throughout all sections
3. THE Personal_Page SHALL load quickly and efficiently
4. THE Personal_Page SHALL be accessible to users with disabilities
